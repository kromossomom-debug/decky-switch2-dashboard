# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-29

- **Authentic Nintendo Switch 2 HOME Dock.** Round shortcut buttons now feature 1:1 vector SVG icons and Nintendo's vibrant signature colors:
  - Nintendo Switch Online (Switch Red), GameChat (Indigo Blue with headset), News (Orange-Red megaphone), Nintendo eShop (Orange shopping bag), Album (Sky Blue photo frame), GameShare (Purple with dual Joy-Cons), Controllers (Joy-Con Slate with Neon Cyan & Neon Red Joy-Cons), Virtual Game Cards (Teal cartridge), System Settings (8-tooth slate gear), and Sleep Mode (Charcoal standby).
- **Switch 2 Visual Effects.**
  - Focus halo with glowing animated multi-color conic rainbow ring.
  - Scale pop animation and press compression on shortcuts.
  - 3D top-glass gloss reflection on circular buttons.
  - Glowing labels in accent colors.
- **Switch 2 Quick Settings Menu (Menu Rápido).**
  - Press **Y** on the HOME Menu to open a Switch 2-style slide-out side panel.
  - Quick adjustments for Brightness and Volume.
  - Fast toggles for Theme (Basic Black / Basic White), All Software, and Instant Sleep Mode.
- **Switch 2 Profile Avatar Customizer.**
  - Replacing the external Steam web link, **User Settings → Change Icon** now opens a built-in Switch 2 Avatar Creator.
  - **21 Authentic Nintendo Character Icons:** Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, Bowser, Yoshi, Toad, Donkey Kong, Triforce, Hylian Crest, Master Sword, Pokéball, Pikachu, Kirby, Warp Star, Screw Attack, Inkling Squid, Animal Crossing Leaf, Switch 2 Logo, Dual Joy-Cons, Retro NES, and Steam Profile.
  - **15 Official Background Colors:** Mario Red, Neon Red, eShop Orange, Pikachu Yellow, Yoshi Green, Luigi Green, Neon Cyan, Sky Blue, Cobalt Blue, Waluigi Purple, Peach Pink, Slate Gray, Dark Charcoal, Pure White, and Golden Star.
  - Live circular preview with ambient Switch 2 glow animation.
  - Custom avatars seamlessly appear across the entire dashboard (status bar, My Page title, profile, and Quick Menu).
  - One-click option to revert to standard Steam avatar.
- **Enhanced Basic White Theme Legibility.**
  - Significantly improved contrast for game titles and shortcut labels using a deep, high-contrast Nintendo Blue (`#00508e`) instead of low-contrast light cyan.
  - Removed muddy text shadows on white backgrounds for sharp, crystal-clear readability.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-25

- **My Page.** Press **A** on your avatar for the console's user page, with a menu on the left that fills the right
  side as you move through it:
  - **Profile:** avatar, name, status, Steam friend code and **Play Activity** (hours played and when you last played).
  - **Friend List:** your friends as round icons, the ones playing first, with what they're playing or when they were
    last online.
  - **Add Friend:** your friend code, search by code, received and sent friend requests.
  - **User Settings:** edit profile, change icon, privacy settings, Steam account.
- White-theme rows and menus use white cards, like the console.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-25

- **All Software screen.** The All Software tile now opens a Switch-style grid of your whole library: square tiles,
  six to a row, with the selected title above it. Games that aren't installed are dimmed with a cloud icon, like
  archived software. **Y** sorts by last played, play time or title; **B** goes back.
- Long titles in the last column end at the tile's edge.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

First release.

- Switch 2-style HOME Menu replacing Steam's home in Game Mode: status bar, a row of square game tiles, ten round
  shortcuts and a footer with button hints.
- Recent games (4–20, default 12) followed by **All Software**. The selected game's name sits above its tile with a
  pulsing cyan frame; long names slide left to stay on screen.
- Round shortcuts in the console's order, mapped to Steam: Steam Online, Chat, News, Steam Store, Album, Remote Play,
  Controllers, Library, System Settings and Sleep Mode. Gray symbols and a rainbow selection ring.
- Live clock, Wi-Fi and battery. Steam's top and bottom bars are hidden on the HOME Menu only.
- Basic Black and Basic White themes, tile art choice (cover, banner or hero) and a 24-hour clock option.
- **A** starts or resumes a game, **☰** opens Steam's game menu.
- English and Portuguese.
