# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-25

- **My Page.** Press **A** on your avatar for the console's user page, with a menu on the left that fills the right
  side as you move through it:
  - **Profile:** avatar, name, status, Steam friend code and **Play Activity** (hours played and when you last played).
  - **Friend List:** your friends as round icons, the ones playing first, with what they're playing or when they were
    last online.
  - **Add Friend:** your friend code, search by code, received and sent friend requests.
  - **User Settings:** edit profile, change icon, privacy settings, Steam account.
- White-theme rows and menus use white cards, like the console.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-25

- **All Software screen.** The All Software tile now opens a Switch-style grid of your whole library: square tiles,
  six to a row, with the selected title above it. Games that aren't installed are dimmed with a cloud icon, like
  archived software. **Y** sorts by last played, play time or title; **B** goes back.
- Long titles in the last column end at the tile's edge.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

First release.

- Switch 2-style HOME Menu replacing Steam's home in Game Mode: status bar, a row of square game tiles, ten round
  shortcuts and a footer with button hints.
- Recent games (4–20, default 12) followed by **All Software**. The selected game's name sits above its tile with a
  pulsing cyan frame; long names slide left to stay on screen.
- Round shortcuts in the console's order, mapped to Steam: Steam Online, Chat, News, Steam Store, Album, Remote Play,
  Controllers, Library, System Settings and Sleep Mode. Gray symbols and a rainbow selection ring.
- Live clock, Wi-Fi and battery. Steam's top and bottom bars are hidden on the HOME Menu only.
- Basic Black and Basic White themes, tile art choice (cover, banner or hero) and a 24-hour clock option.
- **A** starts or resumes a game, **☰** opens Steam's game menu.
- English and Portuguese.
